# Stratigraphic support

Run only from a sealed `agentlas.paleontology-catalog-result/v1` and retain its parent ResearchRun identity and record hashes. Compare preserved older/younger age bounds and named stratigraphic units. Generate a row-level publication table and interval-bar Vega figure with units, provenance, truncation state, assumptions, and missingness. Any midpoint is a visualization summary only and cannot be analyzed as a point observation.

Do not infer morphology, DNA, genomes, viable embryos, hatching, or resurrection feasibility. Send the resulting artifact receipts and evidence boundary to the Research Director before manuscript use.
