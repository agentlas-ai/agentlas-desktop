description: Calculate explicit retirement, income, allocation and rebalancing scenarios.
---

Use plan for deterministic cashflow, income-capital goals and target-weight rebalancing. Obtain material assumptions first; do not infer return, tax, currency, fees or legal plan rules from nationality. Pension and 401(k) scenarios require the native owner-reviewed offered menu and restrictions. A target-weight plan is not an income-portfolio optimizer or a trade. Use calculate for actual aligned-history allocation, bootstrap scenarios and explicit option assumptions. Inspect returned computed values, status and limitations before report_text writes the conclusion into the saved artifact. A depletion chart is not a probability of retirement success.
