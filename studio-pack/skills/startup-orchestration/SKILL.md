# Startup Orchestration

Use this skill when a founder asks to validate, plan, research, specify, or build a startup.

## Routing

- Raw idea, problem, customer, revenue model -> `Startup/01-idea-foundry-hq`
- Market, competitors, persona feedback, differentiation -> `Startup/02-market-intelligence-hq`
- Business plan, Word-ready plan, financial story -> `Startup/03-business-plan-hq`
- Product spec, PRD, UX flow, wireframes -> `Startup/04-product-planning-prd-maker`
- App/web/game/backend/login/payment/build plan -> `Startup/05-product-development-hq`
- Pitch deck, IR deck, PPT, market deck, sales deck, investor update -> `Startup/06-pitch-deck-ir-hq`

## Rule

Keep founder-facing output practical:

- decision
- evidence
- risk
- artifact
- next action
