# 03 Business Plan

## Positioning

Startup Studio is the founder operating board for going from idea to build-ready
startup package.

## Offer

In one guided flow, the founder gets:

- idea pressure test;
- market and competitor scan;
- business plan draft;
- PRD and screen spec;
- Google Stitch design handoff;
- app and web build plan;
- QA and launch checklist.

## Revenue Model

Primary revenue is subscription. Secondary revenue is paid concierge review,
artifact export packs, and startup-program templates.

## Package Offer

| Package | Price hypothesis | Promise | Proof needed |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Starter | $19-29/month | Validate one idea and export a basic Founder Packet | 3 paid solo-founder starts |
| Studio | $79-149/month | Run multiple ideas with Stitch handoff and app/web proof | 3 retained founders using a second idea |
| Concierge | $299-999/run | Reviewed support-program or investor-ready package | 3 paid reviewed runs with before/after artifacts |

The webapp must let the founder select a package and carry that choice into the
Founder Packet. This is a purchase-intent signal only; real payment processing
is outside the current proof.

## Distribution

1. Hephaestus/Agentlas public demo.
2. Korean startup communities and indie-builder channels.
3. Startup support-program content: "turn a rough idea into a 지원사업-ready package."
4. Google Stitch workflow content: "design-ready PRD in one pass."
