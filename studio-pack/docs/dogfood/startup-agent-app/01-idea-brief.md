# 01 Idea Brief

## Scope Sentence

Early founders use Startup Studio to turn a raw startup idea into a validated
business, PRD, design, app/web build plan, and launch checklist; the product
makes money through a subscription plus paid artifact export packs.

## Business Idea Engine Pass

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Target customer | Solo founder, indie builder, early startup CEO, startup-program applicant |
| Urgent problem | They have an idea but cannot turn it into credible evidence, product scope, and a buildable first version |
| Current alternative | General AI chat, Notion templates, consultants, accelerators, scattered Figma/PRD/doc tools |
| Korea saturation | High for generic AI assistants; lower for Korean-first founder workflow that ties business plan, PRD, design, and build QA |
| Marketing dependence | High unless distribution is anchored in founder communities, startup support programs, and Agentlas/Hephaestus demos |
| Moat candidate | Packaged multi-HQ workflow, durable project memory, rendered artifact QA, public agent distribution, localized Korean startup docs |
| Fast evidence | 10 founder interviews, 3 paid concierge runs, 1 public demo, 1 Stitch-generated design handoff |

## Verdict

`BUILD`

Reason: the pain is real and frequent, but the product must prove end-to-end
artifact quality quickly. A generic "ask AI about your startup" positioning is
too weak; the sellable angle is "from idea to build-ready app package with
visible artifacts."

## Weekend MVP

1. Ship lifecycle UI with right app/web artifact dock.
2. Run one dogfood startup package from idea to Stitch prompt and build plan.
3. Record every broken route, vague copy, missing provider step, and visual bug.
4. Publish a founder-facing demo script and one before/after case study.
